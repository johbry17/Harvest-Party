# Queridos Amigos,

While we can’t bring about the end of the government shutdown, we could definitely end your wait for the most anticipated annual fall event in Northeast DC, North of H St., East of North Capitol Street and South of Florida Ave!

You are cordially invited to join us **Saturday, October 25th, 2025** from 12:00 PM – 10:00 PM for the latest edition of **HARVEST PARTY**! This **DAYTIME PARTY** will feature lively tunes, libations, sweet dance moves, and lovely people. You may even find the love of your life – or the like of your next two weeks!

As usual, we will have a hard stop at 10 – to avoid any and all of DC’s occupying forces.

  <ul>Beer (Fishbowl Brewing Co.’s signature homebrews and barrels of local craft brew)</ul>

  <ul>A fucking good time</ul>
